# Citation & reference rules — ACM (acmart)

**Document class:** `acmart` (e.g. `[sigconf]` for conference proceedings).
**Bibliography style:** `ACM-Reference-Format` (`\bibliographystyle{ACM-Reference-Format}`).

## In-text citations
- **Numeric** by default, square brackets, via `\cite{key}` → `[7]`.
- Multiple: `\cite{a,b,c}` → `[1, 4, 9]`.
- `acmart` also defines `\citeauthor{key}`, `\citeyear{key}`, `\citet{key}` and
  `\Citet{key}` (it loads `natbib`) for when you must weave authors into the
  sentence — but the bracketed `[7]` form stays numeric. Don't switch the whole
  document to author-year.
- Don't use `[7]` as a sentence subject; write "the method of Vaswani et al.
  [7]" or "\citet{vaswani2017attention} showed…".

## Reference list
- Auto-generated by BibTeX from `references.bib`; numbered, ordered by first
  citation. Do not hand-edit it.
- ACM-Reference-Format wants complete metadata: full author names, full
  venue/series names, year, pages, **DOI** (it renders `https://doi.org/…`).
  Missing fields produce ugly entries and reviewer complaints.
- arXiv preprints → `@misc` with `eprint` + `archivePrefix = {arXiv}`.

## Pitfalls the citation-qa check looks for
- `\cite{key}` with no entry in `references.bib`.
- Entries in `references.bib` never cited.
- A reference to a paper not in the strata library (invented citation).
- An all-author-year document (acmart proceedings are numeric).
- Hand-numbered `[3]` markers / a hand-written reference list.
- Missing CCS concepts / `\keywords` (required for ACM submissions; the skeleton
  flags them with `TODO`).
